# Upper cabinets over a run: what was approved

**Status: approved by Max and built.** The rules are now section W of [RULES.md](RULES.md). His answers are at the end.

## Splitting stage 3

Stage 3 was "corners, uppers over a run, and a room". That is three separate pieces of work, so I suggest taking them one at a time, each reviewed before the next:

1. **Upper cabinets over a run** (this proposal). The same wall gets a row of uppers above the base cabinets.
2. **Corners.** A second run on the next wall, meeting the first at a corner.
3. **A room.** Walls, windows, doors and plumbing on a floor plan.

## The idea

A kitchen wall has two rows: base cabinets on the floor and upper cabinets on the wall above. Today a run is only the bottom row.

With this change the app adds the top row for her. It lines each upper cabinet up with what is underneath, leaves gaps where there should be none, and adds the uppers to the same shopping list, cutting diagrams and build.

Each upper cabinet is built by the rules already approved (rule K7 and the rest). This proposal covers only how the row is laid out and hung.

## W1. The app proposes the top row

She is not asked for anything new. The top row follows the bottom row:

| Underneath | Above it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Base cabinet or drawer base | An upper cabinet, the same width | Doors line up top and bottom |
| Dishwasher space | An upper cabinet, the same width | The dishwasher is under the counter |
| Sink base | Nothing | Most sinks sit under a window |
| Range space | Nothing | Room for a hood |
| Fridge space | Nothing | The fridge fills the height |

Each of these is a choice she can change: any upper can become a gap, and any gap an upper.

## W2. Widths follow the bottom row

An upper is exactly as wide as what is under it, so the edges line up. There is nothing to share out and nothing to add up: the top row is the same length as the bottom row by construction.

## W3. Size and height on the wall

- Every upper in the row shares one depth and one height, starting at 12" and 30" (rule K1). She can change both.
- The bottom of the uppers sits 18" above the countertop. That is a default she can change.
- Height of the bottom of the uppers above the floor = platform height + base box height + countertop thickness + 18". With the defaults that is 3 1/2" + 31" + 1 1/2" + 18" = 54".
- The app checks that the top of the uppers is no higher than 96" (a default for ceiling height) and warns if it is.

## W4. Each upper is its own box, screwed to its neighbours

The same as the bottom row (rule R3): each upper is built complete, then screwed to the next through the frame pieces where they meet. The frame overhang applies only at a wall end of the run. A side beside a gap can be seen, so it is treated as a finished end.

## W5. Numbers

Upper cabinets are numbered left to right: U01, U02. Their parts are U01-01, U01-02. Base cabinets keep C01, C02.

## W6. Hanging the row

One ledge board for the whole row, screwed level to the wall at the height from W3. The uppers are hung from one end, each screwed to the wall through its two mounting strips and to the cabinet before it. Then the ledge comes off.

The app says this is a two-person job at every lift.

## W7. One shopping list, shared sheets

Uppers go on the same cutting diagrams as the base cabinets, and into the same shopping list and tool list (rule R6).

## W8. Building order

All base cabinets are built first, then all uppers, each up to its finish. Then the base run is set in place, then the uppers are hung, then drawers, doors and handles are fitted everywhere. Hanging the uppers after the base cabinets are in means lifting over them; the alternative is to hang the uppers first, which is easier to reach but leaves nothing to measure up from. I have chosen base first, uppers second.

## Not in this stage

- Corners and a second wall.
- A short cabinet over the fridge or the range.
- Open shelves and glass doors.
- Trim along the top of the uppers.

## What changes in the app

- The Run screen shows both rows in its drawing, to scale, with the gap between them.
- Under the bottom row's list there is a short "Above" list: for each position, "Upper cabinet" or "Nothing", with a button to switch.
- Depth and height of the uppers, their height above the counter, and the ceiling height appear on the Choices screen with the other defaults.
- Parts, Cutting, Shop and Build cover both rows.

## Test case

The same made-up 120" wall: a drawer base, a sink base, a dishwasher space and a base cabinet. By W1 that gives three uppers (over the drawer base, the dishwasher space and the base cabinet) and a gap over the sink.
