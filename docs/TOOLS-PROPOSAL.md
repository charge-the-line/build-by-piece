# Tool inventory: approved proposal

**Status: approved by Max with changes A to D, and built.** The rules now live in [RULES.md](RULES.md), section T (with D8 to D10 and step A10). This page records what was proposed, what changed on approval, and the answers.

## Changes made on approval

| | Change | Where it landed |
|---|---|---|
| A | New default for framed fronts when a router is ticked: build the frame, rout a recess round the back, glue the panel in. The stopped groove stays as an advanced choice. | Rules T5, D9 |
| B | Panel on the back: a rule and a test that the hinges always have a clear place to mount. The panel is kept back from the hinge edge by the space the hinge needs. | Rules D8, D10 |
| C | "Tools for this plan" names the exact router bit for the chosen method. The recess depth and the groove width follow the measured panel thickness, which is checked at the cutting step. | Rule T2 |
| D | Jigsaw and hole saw added to the tool list for plumbing openings, with a new step. | Rules T1, A10 |

## Answers

1. **Tools on hand:** circular saw, mitre saw, drill and driver, pocket-hole jig, brad nailer, sander, clamps, jigsaw. Router: buying, so it is ticked. No table saw, no track saw. These are the default ticks.
2. **Pocket-hole jig stays required.** Without one the app says "buy or borrow" and keeps the joints.
3. **No router and no table saw:** panel on the back, subject to change B.
4. **Track saw:** lay out both ways and keep whichever needs fewer sheets; sections first on a tie.
5. **Table saw:** board-as-sold widths stay the defaults; only the lengthwise-cut warning is dropped.
6. **Left out of this round:** the router recess for the back panel and grooved drawer bottoms.
7. **Safety:** glasses, ear protection and a dust mask are listed as required on every plan.

## Two readings to confirm

- **"Groove and recess width follow the measured panel thickness."** For the groove, its *width* is the panel thickness. For the recess, it is the *depth* that matches the panel thickness (so the panel sits flush with the back); how far the recess reaches into the frame is a separate default of 3/8".
- **A panel on the back of a framed drawer front** needs somewhere to go, so with that method every drawer box sits back from the front of the face frame by the panel's thickness (rule X6).
